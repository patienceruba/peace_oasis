Payments

Simple, Transparent Pricing

Our two-stage payment model keeps your booking secure while minimizing upfront commitment. No hidden fees — what you see is what you pay.

  • Prices displayed in RWF, USD, and EUR based on your location
  • 10% deposit locks in your room instantly
  • Remaining 60% paid only when you arrive
  • Automatic cancellation if no check-in within 24 hours of arrival
  • Secure payment processing with full encryption
  • Cancellation policies outlined clearly during booking
